DIGITAL PROJECT GUIDE

Make the brief clear before production begins

Digital and print work is more effective when the intended audience, purpose, technical requirements and approval process are agreed in advance. NGP Integrated Services Limited combines ICT, digital communication, graphic design, printing and signage support so organisations can coordinate related requirements through one enquiry.

Websites and digital platforms

Start with the audience, the actions visitors should take, the pages and information required, who will update the content and how enquiries should be handled. A project brief should also cover mobile usability, accessibility, security, privacy, analytics, domain ownership and who is responsible for future maintenance.

Corporate graphics and print

Supply the approved logo and brand colours, final wording, target dimensions, intended use, quantity, preferred material and delivery deadline. Proofread names, phone numbers, email addresses, web addresses and dates before authorising production. Ask for a proof where layout or colour accuracy matters.

Signage requirements

Record the installation location, viewing distance, indoor or outdoor exposure, approximate dimensions, mounting surface, visibility needs and any site permissions. The choice of material and fixing method should match the environment and expected service life.

How to prepare a useful enquiry

  1. Explain the objective. Describe the business problem, audience or operational task the project should support.
  2. Provide source materials. Share approved copy, brand assets, existing specifications, dimensions or examples that clarify the expected outcome.
  3. Confirm constraints. State the budget range if available, delivery date, location, technical dependencies and approval contact.
  4. Agree deliverables. Confirm file formats, quantities, installation needs, testing, revisions and handover before work starts.

Quality and handover checks

For websites, review navigation, mobile display, links, forms, page titles, privacy information and analytics setup before launch. For print and signage, verify spelling, dimensions, colour proof, material, quantity and installation details. For technology support, record the issue, affected users, system or device, troubleshooting already attempted and any business impact. Keep a record of the agreed scope and final approved assets.

Digital privacy and security

Collect only information needed for a stated purpose, restrict access to business accounts, use strong unique credentials and enable multi-factor authentication where available. Website owners should retain control of domain, hosting, analytics and advertising accounts, and ensure privacy notices accurately describe the tools and data practices in use. Avoid placing passwords or sensitive personal information in public enquiry forms.
