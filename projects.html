PROJECT DELIVERY FRAMEWORK

From a clear brief to a documented handover.

NGP organises project work around the agreed outcome and the practical conditions of the site. The portfolio categories on this page describe our service capabilities and areas of project interest; they should not be read as a claim that every listed project type has been completed for a named client.

1. Define

Record the client's objective, scope boundaries, location, stakeholders, required outputs, constraints and acceptance criteria.

2. Plan

Identify work packages, competent resources, materials, dependencies, access arrangements, schedule assumptions and project risks.

3. Coordinate

Track actions, approvals, procurement interfaces, site activities, changes and client communications against the agreed plan.

4. Verify and hand over

Review completed outputs against the agreed scope, record outstanding items, provide available operating information and document handover.

Project information and references

For a specific procurement or partnership opportunity, NGP can assess the requested project-reference format and identify what supporting evidence is available and authorised for disclosure. Client names, contract values, completion certificates, photographs and performance claims are only published or shared where they are accurate and permission allows.
