CONSULTANCY METHOD

From a business question to an actionable plan

Useful consultancy begins with a defined decision or problem. NGP works with clients to clarify the objective, gather relevant information, compare feasible options and identify practical next steps across technology, infrastructure, operations and project development.

Areas we can help structure

  • Project scoping: defining needs, work packages, dependencies, milestones and acceptance criteria.
  • Technology planning: documenting current systems, user requirements, connectivity considerations and options for improvement.
  • Operational improvement: mapping workflows, identifying avoidable hand-offs and preparing a more consistent process.
  • Partnership and proposal development: organising objectives, stakeholder roles, implementation phases, resource needs and measurable outputs.
  • Training and capability planning: identifying skills gaps and aligning practical learning with intended work.

What clients should prepare

To make an initial discussion productive, share the problem to be solved, who will use the outcome, any existing plans or specifications, the location, the target timeline and known constraints. Sensitive documents should be shared only through an agreed and appropriate channel.

Expected outputs

Depending on the agreed scope, outputs may include a requirements summary, options comparison, project brief, implementation roadmap, risk register, draft proposal or recommendation report. These are scoped and priced according to the work involved; this page does not represent a promise of a free audit or a fixed deliverable.
