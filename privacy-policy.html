Privacy Policy

NGP Integrated Services Limited (RC 7738832) respects the privacy of people who visit our website or contact our business. This notice explains the types of information that may be handled, why it may be used and how you can contact us about it.

1. Information you provide

When you contact NGP, you may choose to provide your name, organisation, telephone number, email address, project location, service requirements, photographs, specifications or other information relevant to an enquiry. Please provide only information reasonably needed for the enquiry.

Do not submit passwords, bank PINs, one-time passcodes, payment-card security details or other authentication secrets through this website, email or ordinary messaging channels.

2. Technical and usage information

Like most websites, this site and its hosting or security services may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, pages requested, approximate visit times, referral information and diagnostic logs. This information may be used to operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website.

3. How information is used

  • To respond to service, quotation, training, project and partnership enquiries.
  • To assess requirements and communicate about a potential or existing business engagement.
  • To maintain website security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • To understand website performance and improve our information and services, subject to applicable consent requirements.
  • To meet legitimate legal, accounting or business record-keeping obligations.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The website uses essential technologies to support its operation. It may also use Google Analytics and Google AdSense or related Google technologies. These services may process technical and usage information and may use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own terms and policies.

The website provides controls to accept optional technologies or reject them. Your choice is stored in your browser using the ngp-consent preference. Choosing “Reject optional” updates the website's Google Consent Mode signals to deny optional analytics and advertising storage. Browser settings may also allow you to clear or restrict stored information. The operation of third-party tags and consent signals can depend on their technical configuration and the visitor's region.

For more information about Google's processing, consult Google's own privacy and advertising disclosures. NGP does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers.

5. Sharing and third-party services

Information may be handled by service providers that support website hosting, security, analytics, advertising, email or communications. If you choose to contact NGP through WhatsApp, your use of that service is also subject to the provider's terms and privacy policy. We do not sell personal information as a business activity.

6. Retention and security

NGP seeks to retain enquiry and business information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, for ongoing business administration, or where a legal or record-keeping obligation applies. Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information; however, no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

Depending on applicable data-protection law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of certain personal information, or raise an objection or complaint. Requests will be considered in light of applicable law, identity verification and any legal duties to retain records.

8. External links

Our website may link to third-party websites or platforms. Those services operate independently and have their own privacy notices, security practices and terms. Review those notices before providing information to them.

9. Children

This website is intended for general business and service information. It is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to NGP through this website, contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our website, services or applicable requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
